The reality

What transport teams deal with every day.

Indian transport and freight businesses run on LR books, WhatsApp groups, driver phone calls and a Tally ledger updated days after the truck returns. Extraaaz Logistics OS puts booking, dispatch, warehouse, documentation and billing on one job record, with Business Operations adding CRM, WMS and finance where you need them.

Status lives on the driver's phone

Customers call ops, ops calls the driver, and nobody can confirm where the consignment is without a round of phone calls.

Billing waits for paper PODs

Trip sheets and PODs come back days later, charges are re-keyed into Tally, and the invoice leaves after the customer has forgotten the job.

E-way bills and documents live outside the job

LRs, e-way bills, BLs and shipping bills sit in folders and inboxes, disconnected from the shipment they belong to.

Margin is known only at month-end

Freight, fuel, toll and vendor costs arrive late, so job-level profit is guessed after the next hundred trips are already moving.

How work moves

The transport operating loop, on one record.

Every step writes to the same record, so the next team starts with what the last one did — not with a phone call.

  1. 01Enquiry

    Quote from rate cards and tariffs

  2. 02Booking

    Booking becomes the live job record

  3. 03Dispatch

    Assign vehicle and driver, plan the trip

  4. 04Delivery

    POD captured on the driver's phone, trip closed

  5. 05Invoice

    GST-ready invoice from actual job charges
